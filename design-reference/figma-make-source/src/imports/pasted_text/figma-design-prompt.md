# Figma Design AI Prompt — 2040 Freelance Technology Studio

## Role

Act as an elite digital art director, senior product designer, interaction designer, brand designer, and conversion-focused UX designer.

Design a complete, production-ready marketing website in Figma for a **two-developer freelance technology studio**.

This is not a generic freelance portfolio.

This is not a traditional agency website.

This is not a SaaS template.

The studio consists of two highly capable software developers combining their experience to win serious freelance projects together. The website must position them as a small, senior, highly technical product engineering studio capable of taking ambitious software projects from concept to production.

The design should feel as though it belongs in **2040** while remaining credible, elegant, professional, usable, and commercially effective today.

The result should feel like a website that could be featured on:

- Awwwards
- Godly
- Land-book
- SiteInspire
- CSS Design Awards

But conversion, usability, credibility, and clarity are more important than visual experimentation.

---

# PRIMARY OBJECTIVE

The website must accomplish five things extremely well:

1. Immediately communicate what the studio does.
2. Establish technical and design credibility.
3. Showcase projects through compelling case studies.
4. Introduce both developers as complementary senior specialists.
5. Convert founders, companies, and decision-makers into project inquiries.

A visitor should quickly understand:

> Two experienced developers. Direct collaboration. Serious software. No agency bureaucracy.

The visual experience should make the studio feel considerably more established and sophisticated than a normal two-person freelance operation.

---

# DESIGN PHILOSOPHY

Use **impeccable taste**.

Apply the interaction and interface philosophy associated with **Emil Kowalski**:

- Precise interaction feedback
- Natural movement
- Thoughtful easing
- Purposeful animation
- Excellent spacing
- Strong hierarchy
- Tactile interfaces
- Micro-interactions that communicate state
- Motion that explains relationships
- Restraint
- Details that make interfaces feel exceptionally polished

Every visual decision must feel intentional.

Do not add effects simply because they look futuristic.

The design should combine:

**technology + editorial design + architecture + cinematic composition + precision engineering + subtle futurism**

Think of the website as a digital environment rather than a collection of stacked website sections.
